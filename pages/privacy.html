Information we collect

This demonstration site does not run a backend and does not collect personal information beyond what your browser sends to any static website by default (such as basic request logs kept by our hosting provider, Netlify). If you submit the newsletter or contact forms on this template, no data is actually transmitted anywhere — they are illustrative only.

Cookies and local storage

This site stores a single value in your browser's local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference. Nothing else is stored, and no data is shared with third parties.
